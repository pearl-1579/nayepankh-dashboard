ℹ️ Our Story

Building a Badalte Bharat

From a few high school students during COVID to one of India's largest youth-led NGOs — here's how NayePankh came to be.

📅 Timeline
Our Journey
2020 — COVID-19
The Spark
High school students begin distributing food and essentials to families during India's lockdown using personal savings and local donations.
March 28, 2021
Official Registration
NayePankh Foundation formally registered under the Indian Society Act, 1860. "NayePankh" — New Wings — chosen to symbolize upliftment.
Aug 2022
12A, 80G & NITI Aayog
Government tax certifications acquired. YouTube channel launched. NITI Aayog affiliation received. Expanded operations to Ghaziabad.
2023
1 Lakh Milestone
100,000+ people helped. Media coverage in Dainik Jagran, Pioneer, and Hindustan. Instagram community crosses 10K.
2024–2026
National Scale
30,000+ interns, 2 lakh+ beneficiaries. 36+ roles on Internshala. Technical Internship Program with 16+ domains launched.
🎯 Mission & Vision
Why We Exist

NayePankh believes that every person deserves dignity, opportunity, and access to basic needs — regardless of economic background. We turn this belief into action through community-led programs.

🎯
Mission
"Badalte Bharat Ki Nayi Tasveer" — Uplift underprivileged communities through food, hygiene, education, and skill development, powered by student volunteers.
🌟
Vision
An India where no child goes to bed hungry, every woman has access to hygiene, and every young person has the skills and confidence to shape their future.
💡
Approach
Youth-led, remote-first, and community-rooted. We leverage India's 30K+ student interns as both workforce and ambassadors for change.

💛 Our Programs

Six Programs, One Mission

Each program is designed to address a specific, urgent need in underprivileged communities — and together they form a comprehensive safety net.

🍱

Food Distribution

Flagship Program Active ✅
85,000+
Meals Served
Weekly
Frequency
42%
Of Total Impact
Our flagship program organises weekly food drives distributing cooked meals and dry ration kits to underprivileged families in Kanpur and surrounding areas. Volunteers mobilise every weekend to ensure no family goes hungry. This program kickstarted the foundation in 2020 during COVID-19 and remains the heart of our work.
🩸

Menstrual Hygiene

Health & Dignity Active ✅
45,000+
Women Reached
Monthly
Campaigns
UP-Wide
Coverage
Monthly campaigns distribute sanitary napkins and conduct hygiene awareness workshops across multiple UP districts. We work to break the taboo around menstruation and ensure every woman has access to safe, affordable hygiene products. This program also targets rural schools and colleges for maximum reach.
👕

Clothes Drive

Basic Needs Active ✅
30,000+

Kanpur + GZB
Seasonal clothing donation drives collect gently-used garments and distribute them during winter and festive occasions. We partner with schools, offices, and resident welfare associations to collect, sort, and redistribute with dignity. No one should face the cold without proper clothing.
